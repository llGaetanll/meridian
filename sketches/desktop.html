Connections Built. Balances and the connections are one page, and the tab is Connections: a bank is a block, and the accounts under it are the ones it brings in, held and owed together. No rail — a column of shares beside a list of the banks they name was the page said twice. The core builds one block per login now, so ViewConnection has no is_debts. The trash on a bank’s line removes it: Plaid stops the Item and its accounts, transactions and streams go with it, asked again in a card under the button first — the frame below. No eye: nothing dispatches MaskToggled on either platform, which is issue 67.
