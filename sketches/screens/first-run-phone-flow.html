Sketches / First run on the phone, the flow

The same road as the desktop one, in the same four parts, and the same order we want rather than the one that is built. Two things differ, and both are Apple's doing: the subscription is sold by StoreKit, so there is no card form and no waiting on a webhook, and there is no window for a browser to block.

The top row is the road. Under a screen, tied to it by a dashed line, are the other states it can be in. The waits are left out: a button reading Opening Plaid… is the screen above it with its button busy. A dashed box is a screen Meridian does not draw.

1. AccountWho you are. One door: an address that exists signs in, one that does not signs up.
2. PaymentWhat it costs. Apple sells it, so there is no card form of ours to draw.
3. BankThe connection. Nothing in the app is a view of anything until this is done.
4. FinishingThe first read, and the way out of first run.
