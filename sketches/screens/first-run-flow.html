Sketches / First run, the flow

The desktop road from the first screen to a bank connected, in four parts. This is the flow we want, not the one that is built. Three things on it are new: Apple and Google beside our own email door, payment settled before the bank is ever mentioned, and the money taken on Stripe's own page rather than on a card form of ours. A fourth is newer still: an email that already has an account with banks on it lands on Welcome back, its banks listed, instead of being walked through payment and Plaid again.

The top row of a part is its road. Under a screen, tied to it by a dashed line, are the other states it can be in. A box with a dashed edge is not our screen: Apple's, Google's and Plaid's are named rather than drawn, because we cannot know what they will show. Stripe's we can, and it is drawn.

The buttons on the paywall are drawn by Stripe's Express Checkout Element, which shows whichever of Link, Apple Pay and Google Pay the browser and the customer actually have, so the list is never fixed. Paywall is what Safari sees and Paywall, no Apple Pay here is what Chrome on a PC sees. PayPal, Amazon Pay and Klarna are the other three the element can show, and all three are out: the support table says they cannot save a payment method while taking a payment, which is the whole of a subscription. We choose the button height, the corner radius, each button’s theme and its wording, and nothing else.
